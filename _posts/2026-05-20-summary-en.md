## [Mozilla Deprecates asm.js in Favor of WebAssembly](https://spidermonkey.dev/blog/2026/05/20/saying-goodbye-to-asmjs.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Mozilla's SpiderMonkey team announced the deprecation of asm.js, signaling the end of its development and optimization in Firefox. This move reflects the industry-wide shift to WebAssembly as the standard for high-performance web applications. Asm.js was a pioneering technology that enabled near-native performance in the browser and paved the way for WebAssembly. Its deprecation marks the end of an era and solidifies WebAssembly as the universal compilation target for the web. Asm.js is a strict subset of JavaScript that allowed C/C++ code to run efficiently in the browser via ahead-of-time optimization. WebAssembly, which supersedes asm.js, offers a compact binary format and better performance, but lacks direct access to some Web APIs and zero-copy buffer sharing.

hackernews · eqrion · May 20, 12:01 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48206340)

**Background**: Asm.js was introduced by Mozilla in 2013 as a response to Google's NaCl and PNaCl, aiming to bring native-like performance to the web. It was a key enabler for projects like Figma, which initially ran a C++ codebase compiled to asm.js. WebAssembly, first released in 2017, has since become the standard, supported by all major browsers.

## [C's Undefined Behavior: More Pervasive Than You Think](https://blog.habets.se/2026/05/Everything-in-C-is-undefined-behavior.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A blog post titled 'Everything in C is undefined behavior' highlights numerous surprising and lesser-known cases of undefined behavior (UB) in C, sparking extensive community discussion. Understanding the breadth of UB in C is critical for writing safe and portable code, especially as compilers increasingly exploit UB for optimizations that can silently break programs. The article points out that even seemingly innocuous operations like reading a volatile variable twice in a printf argument list can be UB due to unsequenced side effects. Community comments add that unaligned pointer casts are UB even without dereferencing.

hackernews · lycopodiopsida · May 20, 06:07 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48203698)

**Background**: Undefined behavior in C means the language specification imposes no requirements on the program's behavior; anything can happen, including crashes or silent data corruption. Compilers often assume no UB occurs and may optimize code in ways that break programs with hidden UB. Common examples include signed integer overflow, buffer overflows, and use-after-free.
